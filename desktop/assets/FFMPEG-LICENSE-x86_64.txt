FFmpeg — licence and source notice (Intel build)
================================================

Anya Tennis bundles a copy of FFmpeg to cut and encode video. FFmpeg is a
separate program: Anya Tennis runs it as its own process and does not link
against it.

Bundled build
-------------

  FFmpeg 9.0.1
  macOS, x86_64 (Intel), statically linked
  Obtained from https://evermeet.cx/ffmpeg/ (ffmpeg-9.0.1.zip)

Licence
-------

This build is configured with --enable-gpl and --enable-version3, so the
resulting binary is covered by the GNU General Public License, version 3.
The full text of GPL version 3 is in COPYING.GPLv3 next to this file.

Note this differs from the Apple silicon build of Anya Tennis, which bundles
a different FFmpeg binary covered by GPL version 2 (see COPYING.GPLv2). The
two architectures use different upstream builds, so their licences differ.

FFmpeg's own source, including the licence headers of every component, is at
https://ffmpeg.org and https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg.
