INSTALLING ON A MAC

  1. Click the Mac download above and wait for it to finish — it's a big file.
  2. Open the downloaded .dmg. A window appears with the Anya Tennis icon and an Applications folder.
  3. Drag Anya Tennis onto Applications. That's the install.
  4. Open it from Applications or Launchpad. Nothing else to install — no Homebrew, no ffmpeg, no Terminal.
You should not see any warning about an unidentified developer on a Mac. The app is signed and notarized with Apple. If macOS does warn you, don't work around it — that means something is wrong with the build. Send a screenshot instead.

INSTALLING ON WINDOWS

  1. Click the Windows download above and run AnyaTennis-Setup.exe.
  2. At "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. See the note above for why that appears.
  3. Follow the installer. It installs for your user only, so there's no administrator prompt.
  4. Open Anya Tennis from the Start menu.

UPDATING

When a newer version is out, Anya Tennis shows a banner at the top of the window the next time you open it. Click Download, quit the app, and install the new one the same way — on a Mac, choose Replace when it asks. Your videos, and everything the app has already worked out about them, are untouched.

QUESTIONS

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. All the processing happens on your computer, and no video, image or frame ever leaves it. There is no server that could receive one: the models and the video tools are inside the app.

Three things do go over the internet, and that is the whole list. Signing in and checking your subscription talks to Google (Firebase) and exchanges your email address and a login token. Paying opens Stripe in your browser — your card details go to Stripe, never to us and never through the app. And on launch it asks GitHub what the latest version number is, with no account and no identifier attached.

Nothing about your matches is ever sent: not filenames, not how many reels you have made, not how long they were. See the privacy policy.

Do I need to be online to use it?

Only occasionally. The app checks your subscription when it starts, and if it can't reach the internet it keeps working for up to 14 days on what it already knows. A reel that is already running never stops because the connection dropped — the check happens at launch, not while it is working.

How do I cancel, and how does the refund work?

Open Account in the top-right of the app. Within 14 days of your first payment there is a Cancel & refund button: press it and your subscription ends immediately and the full amount goes back to your card, usually within a few business days. No email, no form, no explaining yourself.

That offer can be used once per account. After the 14 days, the same Account screen has Manage subscription, which opens Stripe where you can cancel at the end of the period you have paid for. You keep access until then.

Why does Windows say the file isn't safe?

Because the installer isn't code-signed yet. SmartScreen warns about any installer it hasn't seen signed by a known publisher — it is a statement about the certificate, not about the file. Click More info → Run anyway. The Mac build is signed and notarized with Apple and shows no such warning; a Windows certificate is in progress.

I was a beta tester. Do I have to pay?

No — you get a free year. Create an account with the same email address you used during the beta and it is applied automatically; you will not be asked for a card.

Why is it so big?

About 330 MB to download, around 2 GB installed. It carries its own copies of the machine-learning models that find the ball and the players, and of the video tools that cut the reel, so you don't have to install anything separately.
