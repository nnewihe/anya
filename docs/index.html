Anya Tennis

Watch your matches in minutes, not hours.

Give it a match video and click the four corners of the court once. It gives you back a reel of just the rallies — no waiting between points, no walking to pick up balls. Everything happens on your own Mac; your video is never uploaded anywhere.

DOWNLOAD FOR MAC

Requires an Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer), macOS 12 or later. About 320 MB.

Intel Macs aren't supported yet. If your Mac is from before late 2020, this build won't run on it. Check Apple menu → About This Mac — it needs to say Apple M1, M2, M3 or M4.

INSTALLING

  1. Click Download for Mac above and wait for it to finish — it's a big file.
  2. Open the downloaded AnyaTennis.dmg. A window appears with the Anya Tennis icon and an Applications folder.
  3. Drag Anya Tennis onto Applications. That's the install.
  4. Open it from Applications (or Launchpad). Nothing else to install — no Homebrew, no ffmpeg, no Terminal.
You should not see any warning about an unidentified developer. The app is signed and notarized with Apple. If macOS does warn you, don't work around it — that means something is wrong with the build. Send a screenshot instead.

UPDATING

When a newer version is out, Anya Tennis shows a banner at the top of the window the next time you open it. Click Download, quit the app, and drag the new one onto Applications the same way — choose Replace when macOS asks. Your videos and everything the app has already worked out about them are untouched.

QUESTIONS

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. All the processing happens on your Mac, and no video, image or frame ever leaves it. The only thing the app sends over the internet is a request asking GitHub what the latest released version number is — no account, no identifier, nothing about you or your matches. You can turn even that off by setting ANYA_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1.

Why is it so big?

About 320 MB to download, around 2 GB installed. It carries its own copies of the machine-learning models that find the ball and the players, and of the video tools that cut the reel, so you don't have to install anything separately.

How long does it take to process a match?

Roughly 1.6 × the length of the clip the first time. A 7-minute 4K clip took about 11 minutes on an M4. Running it again on the same video is much faster — it remembers what it already worked out.

Something went wrong. What do you need from me?

Two things: the version number, shown in the top-right corner of the app window, and the log file. You'll find the log in Finder via Go → Go to Folder… and pasting ~/Library/Logs/Anya Tennis. Send app.log along with what you were doing when it happened.

What about Windows?

Not yet. A Windows build is planned but hasn't been tested — this beta is Mac only.
