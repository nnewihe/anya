Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

The short version. Your videos never leave your computer. We hold your email address and your subscription status, and that is essentially all. We do not see your card number, we do not track what you do inside the app, and we do not sell anything to anybody.

WHO WE ARE

Anya Tennis is operated by Andy Nnewihe. You can reach us at nnewihe@gmail.com about anything on this page, including any of the requests below.

YOUR VIDEOS NEVER LEAVE YOUR COMPUTER

This is the important one, so it gets its own section. Anya Tennis does all of its work — finding the ball, tracking the players, working out where points start and end, cutting the reel — on your own machine, using models and video tools that ship inside the app. There is no upload step and no server that could receive a video.

We never receive your footage, single frames from it, filenames, how many reels you have made, how long they were, or anything else about the content of your matches. The files the app writes alongside your video stay on your disk.

WHAT LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER, AND WHEN

  • Signing in and checking your subscription. Your email address, your password (or a Google sign-in), and a login token go to Google's Firebase Authentication. The app then asks our server whether your subscription is current. This happens when you sign in and once when the app launches.
  • Paying. The app opens Stripe's own checkout page in your browser. Your card details are typed into Stripe's page and go to Stripe. They never pass through the app and we never see them.
  • The update check. On launch the app asks GitHub for the latest released version number. It is an ordinary anonymous web request with no account and no identifier attached — GitHub learns that some computer asked for a public page. You can switch it off entirely by setting ANYA_NO_UPDATE_CHECK=1.

WHO ELSE IS INVOLVED

  • Google (Firebase). Holds your account and your subscription status on our behalf. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • Stripe. Takes the payment and holds your billing details. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • GitHub. Hosts the download and this page, and answers the anonymous version check.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and no one outside this list receives it.

LOGS ON YOUR OWN MACHINE

The app writes a diagnostic log to ~/Library/Logs/Anya Tennis on your Mac. It stays there and is never sent anywhere automatically — it only reaches us if you choose to email it when reporting a problem. It records what the app was doing and any errors, and it deliberately does not record sign-in tokens. It may contain the file paths of videos you processed, so glance at it before sending if that matters to you.

Your signed-in session is stored separately, in ~/Library/Application Support/Anya Tennis, precisely so it is not in the folder people are asked to email.

YOUR RIGHTS

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. If you are in the UK, EU, or a US state with its own privacy law, those requests are rights you have rather than favours we do; you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. Email nnewihe@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your email address and account record. We keep the fact that a refund was issued, and Stripe keeps its own record of the transaction, because we are both required to keep financial records.

CHILDREN

Anya Tennis is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

CHANGES

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, we will say so in the app's release notes and update the date at the top. The previous versions of this page are in the repository's history.
